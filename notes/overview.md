# Summary
	- The basic research area is **information retrieval**
	- the goal of information retrieval is returning **relevant documents**
	- Relevancy is somewhat subjective, but relevant documents can be labeled before an experiment as a way of getting an objective metric.
	- A benchmarks of a search engine include: 1) precision, 2) recall, 3) query time, and 4) how the inverted index scales (corpus size v. inverted index size).
	- Precision and Recall are metrics related to the quality of your search engine.
	- Query time and Scalability are related to performance of your search engine.
	- I hope to show the algorithm BM25 improves Precision and Recall
	- I hope to show partitioning improves Query Time
	- I hope to show the inverted index scales linearly with corpus size.
